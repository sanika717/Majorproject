# ESG Scoring Methodology

## Overview

UrbanPulse ESG scoring is **100% deterministic** — no LLM or machine learning is involved
in the calculation of any score, index, or metric. All formulas use official government
standards (CPCB, KSPCB, BBMP) and are verifiable from primary source publications.

---

## 1. Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) National Air Quality Index (NAQI)

### Sub-Index Formula

For each pollutant concentration C:

```
I = I_Lo + (I_Hi - I_Lo) × (C - B_Lo) / (B_Hi - B_Lo)
```

Where `B_Lo`, `B_Hi` are concentration breakpoints and `I_Lo`, `I_Hi` are index breakpoints.

### PM2.5 Breakpoints (µg/m³)

| B_Lo | B_Hi | I_Lo | I_Hi |
|------|------|------|------|
| 0    | 30   | 0    | 50   |
| 31   | 60   | 51   | 100  |
| 61   | 90   | 101  | 200  |
| 91   | 120  | 201  | 300  |
| 121  | 250  | 301  | 400  |
| 251  | 350  | 401  | 500  |

### PM10 Breakpoints (µg/m³)

| B_Lo | B_Hi | I_Lo | I_Hi |
|------|------|------|------|
| 0    | 50   | 0    | 50   |
| 51   | 100  | 51   | 100  |
| 101  | 250  | 101  | 200  |
| 251  | 350  | 201  | 300  |
| 351  | 430  | 301  | 400  |
| 431  | 500  | 401  | 500  |

### AQI Categories

| Score Range | Category |
|-------------|----------|
| 0–50        | Good |
| 51–100      | Satisfactory |
| 101–200     | Moderate |
| 201–300     | Poor |
| 301–400     | Very Poor |
| 401–500     | Severe |

**Composite AQI = max(Sub-Index_PM2.5, Sub-Index_PM10)**
Dominant pollutant = whichever sub-index is higher.

**Source:** CPCB National Air Quality Index, 2014. `EsgEngine.calculate_cpcb_aqi()`.

## 2. ESG Composite Score

### Pillar Weights

| Pillar | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Environmental | 40% |
| Social | 35% |
| Governance | 25% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

### Environmental Pillar (0–100)

| Component | Weight | Formula |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Air Quality | 40% | `max(0, 100 - AQI × 0.20)` |
| Lake Water Quality | 35% | `max(0, 100 - (BOD - 2) × 10)` |
| Energy Efficiency | 25% | Normalized from BESCOM kWh data |

### Social Pillar (0–100)

| Component | Weight | Formula |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Complaint Resolution Rate | 40% | `resolved / total × 100` |
| Public Safety Index | 35% | `max(20, 100 - Crime_Risk_Score)` |
| Civic Infrastructure | 25% | Derived from BBMP project categories |

### Governance Pillar (0–100)

| Component | Weight | Formula |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Contractor Diversity | 40% | Herfindahl-Hirschman Index inversion |
| Expenditure Transparency | 35% | % of work orders with public disclosure |
| Audit Compliance | 25% | Provenance completeness ratio |

### Grade Thresholds

| Score | Grade |
|-------|-------|
| ≥ 85  | A+    |
| ≥ 75  | A     |
| ≥ 65  | B+    |
| ≥ 55  | B     |
| ≥ 45  | C     |
| < 45  | D     |

## 3. Source Attribution

Every metric is tagged with one of three attribution levels:

| Attribution | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| `actual` | Direct verified government sensor or official record |
| `estimated` | Statistically interpolated or empirical proxy |
| `unavailable` | Sensor offline or data missing — never fabricated |

**ZeroResidual Principle:** When data is unavailable, it is explicitly disclosed as `unavailable`.
No value is ever invented, interpolated without disclosure, or generated by an LLM.
