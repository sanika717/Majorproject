Formula

Air = min over pollutants of 100 × min(1, CPCB annual limit ÷ estimated concentration). Concentration at the area centre is the inverse-distance-squared weighted mean of KSPCB station annual means (2016-17). The worst pollutant governs, as in AQI. Air is only scored if a station is within 10 km.

Water = mean points of all monitored lake/tank station-months in the area (KSPCB MINARS 2020-21), with CPCB class points A=100, B=80, C=60, D=40, E=20.

Environmental = equal-weight mean of Air and Water (missing ones are dropped, never guessed).

Overall ESG = 0.4·E + 0.3·S + 0.3·G, re-normalised over the pillars that have data. Social and Governance have no area-level dataset registered yet, so right now Overall ESG = Environmental. Add the dataset and they join the formula automatically.

Confidence: High = station within 6 km and 20+ water station-months; Medium = one of the two is strong; Low = air not scored.

Area centres are approximate, used only for distance. Data years differ per indicator (air 2016-17, water 2020-21), and each is shown as its own period.
