UrbanPulse Construction Data Transparency & Verification Standard

All figures above are computed directly from bbmp-2025-26-198-wards-work-orders.csv (4,729 work-order records) using the cleaning, aggregation, and K-Means++ clustering logic in notebooks/construction/urbanpulse__edaipynb.py. Nothing is invented, estimated, or backfilled. The dataset's unrelated Bangalore-traffic/kagglehub sections were out of scope for this integration and were not run. All 198 official BBMP wards are represented, plus 11 additional non-standard ward codes present in the raw data (e.g. "o001") whose meaning is not specified in the source and is not guessed at here. Work-order category tags (drain, road, maintenance, development, water) are keyword matches on free-text descriptions, so a single work order can be tagged more than once — these do not sum to the total work order count. No BBMP ward-boundary or centroid coordinate dataset has been supplied, so ward markers/polygons are not plotted on the Smart City Map; see the floating panel on the Construction map layer above for the same verified overview figures.

The crime numbers presented above are sourced directly from the official Bengaluru City Police Annual Reports, the verified D47 crime statistics series, and the Bengaluru City Police Press Note (2021-2023). The "All Recorded Crimes" overview reflects the verified 2014-2021 (Up to Dec) D47 historical aggregate; a year-by-year 2021-2023 breakdown of that overall total is EDA In Progress. The Women, Children, Cyber Crime, Suicides, and Accidental Deaths tabs DO have verified 2021-2023 annual breakdowns. All statistics reflect city-level or police-division-level aggregates.
